Privacy Policy

Last Revised: July 2026

Introduction

Thank you for playing SpinWhisker. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

Local Data

SpinWhisker stores your game progress, settings, achievements and other gameplay-related information locally on your device. This data is used only to provide game functionality and is not transmitted to us.

Information Collection

At this time, SpinWhisker does not collect, store, process or share any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or precise location.

User Accounts

SpinWhisker does not require users to create an account.

Third-Party Services

The current version of SpinWhisker does not use:

  • Advertising services
  • Analytics services
  • User tracking
  • Social network integrations
  • Cloud services

Future versions of the game may optionally integrate services such as:

  • Google Play Games (Achievements, Leaderboards and Cloud Save)
  • Google AdMob (Advertisements)
  • Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)

If any of these services are added, this Privacy Policy will be updated before they become available.

Data Sharing

Since SpinWhisker currently does not collect personal information, there is no personal data to sell, rent or share with third parties.

Children's Privacy

SpinWhisker is intended for players of all ages. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as new features or third-party services are added. Any significant changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Revised" date above.

Contact
